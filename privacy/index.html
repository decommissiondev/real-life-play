Marsal privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026. This covers the Marsal app on iOS, Android and the web.

The short version

Marsal doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. This version of the game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your game save

Your character, companies and progress are saved only on your own device (in the app's local storage, or your browser's storage on the web). They are never uploaded. Deleting the app, clearing its data or using "Delete saved game" in Settings removes them permanently.

Feedback you choose to send

The version in the app stores has no feedback screen. Test versions shared with playtesters have a "Send feedback" screen, which prepares a message and opens your device's share sheet or email app. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself, to a recipient you choose. If you leave the "anonymous game snapshot" switch on, the message includes game statistics only (in-game date, generation, net worth, number of companies), never your character's name. Feedback you send is used only to improve the game and is not shared with anyone else.

Purchases

This version of Marsal has no purchases. All money in the game is fictional.

Children

Marsal does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software. The app stores and the website host (GitHub Pages) may process technical data such as IP addresses under their own privacy policies when you download or open the app.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle personal data (for example online play with accounts), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/decommissiondev/real-life-play/issues.
